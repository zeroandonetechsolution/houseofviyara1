SHIPPING & DELIVERY

We are committed to delivering your orders safely and on time. This policy outlines our shipping and delivery procedures.

1. DELIVERY LOCATIONS

We currently ship to all major pin codes across India. International shipping is coming soon!

2. ORDER PROCESSING

Orders are processed within 1-2 business days (excluding weekends and public holidays). Once your order is shipped, you will receive a tracking number via email and SMS.

3. ESTIMATED DELIVERY TIMES

Delivery times vary depending on your location:

  • Metro cities: 3-5 business days
  • Other locations: 5-7 business days

4. SHIPPING CHARGES

We offer free shipping on all orders above ₹999. For orders below ₹999, a flat shipping fee of ₹100 is applicable.

5. SHIPPING PARTNERS

We partner with reputed courier services like Bluedart, Delhivery, and India Post to ensure your orders reach you safely and on time.

6. DELIVERY DELAYS

While we strive to deliver your order on time, there may be delays due to unforeseen circumstances like weather conditions, holidays, or logistical issues. In such cases, we will keep you informed about the status of your order.
