ABOUT US

OUR STORY

Founded in 2023, House Of Viyara was born out of a passion for beautiful, comfortable, and affordable fashion. We believe that everyone deserves to look and feel their best without breaking the bank.

WHAT WE DO

At House Of Viyara, we curate and sell a wide range of women's clothing including kurtis, kurti sets, maxis, cord sets, and pure cotton wear. Our collection is designed to blend traditional Indian aesthetics with modern comfort and style.

OUR MISSION

Our mission is to make quality fashion accessible to everyone. We are committed to providing our customers with the best shopping experience, from browsing our collection to receiving their orders at their doorstep.

OUR VALUES

  • Quality: We never compromise on the quality of our products.
  • Affordability: We believe great fashion shouldn't cost a fortune.
  • Customer First: Our customers are at the heart of everything we do.
  • Sustainability: We are committed to reducing our environmental impact.
